# Legacy & records — the Trophy Room (retention capstone)

**Status:** design spec. The emotional long-tail that makes the dynasty *worth* building. Companion to
`offline-shell-design.md` and `growth-and-content-strategy.md`.

## Purpose
Turn effort into **pride and long-term goals.** This is where "the players *you* made" become a legacy you
want to grow — the payoff that keeps someone playing 50+ hours and *screenshotting their game*.

## What it holds
1. **Trophy cabinet** — leagues, cups, promotions, per season, as you climb the tier pyramid.
2. **Hall of Fame** — the great players *you* developed (the legend cards already built), each with their
   story: peak stats, trophies, awards, and the bloodline they belong to.
3. **🌳 Bloodline trees — THE CENTREPIECE.** A visual family tree of a dynasty: the founder you scouted →
   each generation's descendant, their peak, their honours, how the genes/position evolved across the line.
   This is the unique hook made *visual and permanent*.
4. **Records** — club records + all-time bests (most goals in a season, longest unbeaten run, milestones).
5. **Individual awards** — Ballon d'Or-type honours your players won.
6. **Manager legacy** — career totals (seasons, trophies, players developed, generations bred, tiers climbed).

## Why it's the retention engine
- **Effort → pride.** Sunk cost becomes emotional payoff — the reason to keep going.
- **The bloodline tree is screenshot-and-share gold** — "look at the 5-generation dynasty I built." Organic
  marketing you don't pay for; your differentiator made shareable.
- **Long-term goals** — fill the cabinet, get a player into the Hall of Fame, build a legendary
  multi-generation bloodline, complete the tier climb.
- **Cosmetic tie-in** — unlock cosmetics via the achievements shown here (feeds monetization, no pay-to-win).

## The one to obsess over: the Bloodline Tree
Trophies and records exist in every management game. **The bloodline tree is uniquely yours** — a living,
growing family of footballers you personally developed across decades. Make it beautiful; make it the
**Steam capsule art**; make it the moment a player thinks *"this is mine."* That single screen sells the
entire dynasty fantasy. Prioritise it above the rest of this screen.

## Build notes
- Mostly **surfacing data you already track** (honours, awards, legend cards, generation/lineage, peak
  overall) into new screens — not new systems.
- The bloodline tree needs a lineage record per token (parent → child chain + each generation's
  peak/honours) — much of this exists (generation, pedigree, legacy cards); wire it into a tree view.
- Static, gorgeous, screenshot-friendly (no live simulation) — cheap to build, high marketing value.
