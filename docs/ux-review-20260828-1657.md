# Football Royalty — First-Player UX & Content Review
_Reviewer: fresh Steam buyer, played menu → academy career (~10 turns), all career tabs, hub, club facilities, settings._
_Build: dev server localhost:5173, 2026-08-28. Viewport 1280×720 (a very common laptop size). No JS console errors the whole run — technically clean._

Severity legend: **blocker** (stops/breaks play) · **major** (real confusion or drains fun) · **minor** (noticeable friction) · **polish** (nice-to-have).

---

## THE HEADLINE PROBLEM (Career "NOW" screen — the core loop)

The whole game hangs on the moment-card loop, and two coupled maths problems make the opening ~20 turns (the entire first impression) feel random and unwinnable. Everything else is comparatively cosmetic.

### 1. [major] A **perfect tag-match still routinely returns "Poor"**, with no visible reason
I read the ask ("stamina"), played the one stamina card, and got **"🎯 Perfect read / ✗ Poor"** — twice in a row on different turns, once even with the flavour "he… dug deep for Lung-buster Run only for it to unravel."
- **Why it hurts:** The onboarding literally promises "read what it needs (the tags), then play the card that fits best — good fits develop him faster and *go better*." When the best possible fit visibly goes badly, the player concludes the tag game is fake and their choices don't matter — the death of a card game.
- **Root cause (real, not vibes):** `shared/src/career.ts:790` `fit()` sums `scenario.demand[tag]`, but `makeScenario` **normalises `demand` to sum to 1** (`career.ts:763`). So a single-tag card's max fit is only that tag's *share* — typically ~0.35–0.5, and lower when the moment spreads demand over 3 tags. Then `success = fit + (rng−0.5)*variance …` (`career.ts:1158`) with `variance ≈ 0.3`. A "perfect" read therefore lands around 0.3–0.6, and the grade bands (`main.ts:2286‑2289`) put Brilliant at ≥0.78, Poor at <0.38. **Perfect play mathematically lands Scrappy/Poor most of the time in the opening stage.**
- **Fix:** Give the chosen card's fit far more leverage over `success` (e.g. don't normalise demand, or add a large flat bonus when `answeredAsk`, or shrink early-stage variance). The player must *feel* that reading the tag paid off at least ~70% of the time, or the mechanic has no teeth.

### 2. [major] The "Rival to chase" is **impossible to keep pace with** and the deficit balloons every turn
The rival gap went `0 → 2 ahead → 5 behind → 7 → 14 → 19 behind` over my run. It grows even on turns I play perfectly.
- **Why it hurts:** The onboarding says "Out-develop him to pull ahead." A new player does everything right and watches "▼ 19 behind" climb relentlessly — reads as "I'm failing and nothing helps." Demoralising, and it's the game's main motivational hook.
- **Root cause:** `shared/src/tokens.ts:96` rival gains a **flat 6–9 points every turn**; you gain `success*8*stakes` (`tokens.ts:95`). At Grassroots `stakes = 1` and `success ≈ 0.5`, so **you earn ~4/turn while the rival banks 6–9/turn** — you fall further behind every single turn by design. (It presumably only becomes winnable later when stakes rise to 2–3, but a first-timer never sees that far.)
- **Fix:** Scale the rival rate by the stage's stakes, or handicap it early, so a well-played Grassroots stage keeps pace. At minimum, don't tell the player to "pull ahead" during a stage where that's arithmetically impossible.

### 3. [major] The required tag frequently has **no matching card in the drawn hand**
A "flair"-only life event dealt me four cards, none of them flair. The deck *has* a flair card (Stepover); it just wasn't drawn.
- **Why it hurts:** The player is told to match the tag, then handed a hand where matching is impossible, forced into a guaranteed sub-optimal play, and given no acknowledgement that the situation was unwinnable. Feels unfair/buggy.
- **Fix:** Either guarantee ≥1 card that touches the demanded tag(s) in each hand, or surface a "no clean answer here — pick the least-bad" framing so the player knows it's intentional.

### 4. [minor] "**🎯 Perfect read**" is shown next to "✗ Poor" and even when the read was NOT perfect
On a flair moment I played a teamwork card and still got the green **"Perfect read"** pill (`main.ts:2280`, `answeredAsk = fit >= bestFit − 0.05`). Pairing a green "Perfect read" with a red "Poor" is self-contradictory to a new player.
- **Fix:** Rename the read pill so it can't read as praise-that-was-wrong (e.g. "Right idea" / "Best available"), and make sure it only fires on an actual tag match, not merely "the best of a bad hand."

### 5. [major] The choice cards sit **below the fold every turn** at 1280×720
On the NOW screen the header + rival + meters + last-result banner + moment text consume the whole viewport, so "The moment falls to him — what does he do?" and the four clickable cards are cut off at the bottom edge on every single turn. Page scrolling barely moves.
- **Why it hurts:** The one thing the player must do each turn — pick a card — is the one thing they can't see without hunting/scrolling. On a laptop this is the default, not an edge case.
- **Fix:** Guarantee the card row is in view when a moment is live (collapse/short-form the result banner and the meters strip, or pin the card row). `main.ts:2217` composes the NOW body — the pre-card block is too tall.

### 6. [minor] The 4 cards are drawn from a **fixed 7-card deck**, so moments feel samey fast
By turn 3 I'd seen Lung-buster Run / Stepover / Crunching Tackle / Hold-up Play recur repeatedly; I only learned they were "his deck" by opening the PLAYER tab. The *scenarios* and *writing* vary nicely, but the *answers* are a small rotating set.
- **Fix:** Tell the player on the NOW screen that these are *his* cards (his identity), so recurrence reads as character, not content-thinness. Consider growing the deck as he develops so the hand visibly evolves.

### 7. [minor] Stat development is invisible in the moment
Outcomes say "developed teamwork," but nothing on the NOW screen shows a number moving. The +2 meter nudges (Coach 50→52) are tiny and unlabelled.
- **Fix:** Float a "+2 Teamwork" / "⚡−4" style tick near the relevant bar on resolve so the choice's effect is *seen*, not just asserted.

### 8. [polish] Header shows **two unexplained numbers** ("6/202" and "★ 23")
The "x/202" carries a "Career score" tooltip, yet a separate "★ N" also climbs with good moments. A new player can't tell which is progress-through-life vs score.
- **Fix:** Label them, or merge. If "/202" is turns-remaining in the whole dynasty, say so.

## Main Menu
1. [polish] Clean and inviting — but for a brand-new player the top button is **"▶ Continue — Royal Dynasty"** (a pre-existing save), pushing "＋ New Game" to second. A true first-timer with no save is fine; with demo saves present, the primary action is ambiguous. Ensure New Game is the visual primary when there's nothing meaningful to continue.
2. [minor] Save rows show a raw locale date ("2026. 8. 28.") and a bare "✕" delete with **no confirm** — one misclick nukes a dynasty. Add a confirm step to destructive delete.

## New Game — Club naming
1. [major] **The name field lies about what it wants.** Placeholder says "name your club," but the helper and the engine actually want *your (manager's) name* and append "'s Club": typing "Ironhill Rovers" yields the double-possessive **"Ironhill Rovers's Club"** everywhere for the rest of the game (hub, matchday, season summaries). `client/src/main.ts:634`.
   - **Fix:** Either change the placeholder to "your name" (and preview "Ironhill's Club"), or let the player name the club directly. The current combination guarantees an awkward name on the very first screen.

## Academy — Prospect selection (3 kids)
1. [minor] You pick from three kids with **only vibe descriptions** ("quick as a whip," "electric feet") — no stars, no stats. Immediately after signing, the same kid suddenly shows **4★ and "pedigree 6%."** So the differentiating info exists but is hidden until *after* the irreversible choice.
   - **Fix:** Either show the star/pedigree on the trial cards (informed choice), or lean fully into "trust your eye" and *don't* reveal a precise 4★ two seconds later — the reveal makes the blind pick feel like withheld info.
2. [minor] "**pedigree 6%**" is never explained. New players won't know if 6% is good or bad (it sounds bad next to 4 stars).

## Academy — Sign / "How his career works" intro
1. [polish] The onboarding panel is genuinely good (Moments, Energy, Money, Relationships, Rival all explained). But it double-stacks two "GOT IT ✕" dismiss boxes at once, which feels redundant.

## Agent selection (8 agents)
1. [minor] Eight agents, each a one-line vibe ("Chases the big stage," "in it for you, not money"), with **no concrete mechanics** and small unexplained glyph icons. The intro says an agent "shapes his whole career (exposure, opportunities, how much he'll want to be paid)" but nothing tells you what *this* pick does differently.
   - **Fix:** One concrete effect per agent (e.g. "+exposure, −loyalty"), even if flavour-wrapped, so the choice feels informed rather than a coin-flip.
2. [minor] [a11y] Agent options render as non-focusable `generic` divs, not buttons — no keyboard support (same for the moment cards and the hub tiles). Screen-reader / keyboard players can't select.

## Career tabs — PLAYER / KIT
1. [minor] PLAYER tab stat abbreviations **PAC / SHO / PAS / TAC / STR / CMP / CRE / LDR** have no legend or tooltips. Football fans guess most; CMP/CRE/LDR are non-obvious. Add hover labels.
2. [polish] PLAYER tab is otherwise strong — OVR→potential, star rating, "forming: Ball-Winner," and the full 7-card deck are all clear and useful. KIT tab's honest "this is a text game, so we keep it to the two details that actually show up in his story" is a nice touch.

## Hub
1. [polish] Well-organised and legible (Player / Club / Dynasty, coins, "Rookie Gaffer," diary line). Note the **double-possessive club name** surfaces here too. Also two money concepts coexist — club coins (🪙 500) on the hub vs the player's career 💷 (0) on the career screen — worth a one-line distinction so they aren't read as the same pool.

## Club Facilities
1. [polish] **Best screen in the build.** Six facilities, each with current effect, explicit "Next:" effect, and cost — exactly the "do I understand what this does?" clarity the moment-cards lack. Use this screen's pattern (show the concrete delta) as the model for the career outcomes.

## Settings
1. [polish] Comprehensive and clear (music/SFX with mute + volume, reduce-motion, CRT toggle, UI scale) with good one-line explanations. [a11y] the toggle switches expose no accessible name in the tree.

## What already works well
- **Writing.** The moment prose is genuinely characterful and situational ("With Dad on the touchline in the cold, arms folded…", "a school report that mentioned football more than maths"). It carries the tone the pixel art promises.
- **Onboarding.** The "How his career works" panel explains every system up front without a wall of tutorial modals.
- **Consequence feedback exists.** Playing a mismatched card gives a distinct red "Against his game / Poor" with a fitting narrative — so the game *can* signal bad choices; the problem is that good choices don't reliably signal good outcomes.
- **PLAYER tab & Club Facilities** are clear, informative, and satisfying — concrete numbers, current-vs-next deltas.
- **Technically clean:** zero console errors across the whole session; settings, tabs, and navigation all responsive.

## Triage order for the owner
1. Rebalance so good reads reliably pay off (#1) and the rival is catchable at Grassroots (#2) — these two decide whether the core loop is fun at all.
2. Guarantee a playable card for the demanded tag / reframe no-win hands (#3).
3. Get the choice cards on-screen every turn (#5).
4. Fix the club-name field (New Game #1) and the contradictory "Perfect read/Poor" pill (#4).
5. The rest are minor/polish.
