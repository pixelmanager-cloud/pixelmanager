# Growth & Content Strategy — the north star

**This is the #1 priority for the game.** Read this before deciding what content to build. Every content
addition — by us or the agents — should be judged against the content bar in §4.

---

## 1. The thesis: the development loop is the cash cow

We don't monetize this game with paywalls or friction. We monetize **love and mastery**. The core loop —

> **grow a player with care → they flourish; grow them without care → they regress**

— and its extension across generations (a bloodline built through skilled management) is a **retention +
attachment machine**:

- You pour hours into developing a player and building a bloodline → you're **emotionally invested**. That
  attachment is what brings people back and opens wallets.
- With depth, developing a player *well* becomes a **skill you get better at** → people who feel mastery
  want more prospects, more bloodlines, and the tools to express them.
- The regression downside creates **real stakes**, driving the daily/weekly return that monetization lives on.

Spend follows engagement, never the reverse. **Content is the flywheel; the store is downstream.** For people
to spend, the game must be *absolutely full of meaningful content.*

## 2. Multi-factor growth = every cycle is unique

Player growth should be shaped by **many interacting factors**, so no two development cycles are the same and
mastering the levers is a genuine, rewarding skill. Even ~10 meaningful levers give combinatorially
near-infinite distinct cycles.

**Already in the game:** card choices, coaches/mentors, agent, personality, physical genes, energy/fatigue,
the six stage-aware relationship meters + consequences, summer focus, lifestyle, season events
(form/injury/new-gaffer), training facilities, captain role.

**Rich new levers to layer (priority backlog fuel):**
- **Club & environment** — club/league quality you develop at; **playing time** (a benched player stagnates);
  **teammate quality** (learn from better players); **tactical system fit**; **loan moves** for minutes.
- **Regimen & body** — training *intensity* (fast growth vs burnout/injury); nutrition/conditioning; recovery
  discipline; **growth spurts** (physical genes maturing at different ages, so timing matters).
- **Life & mind** — deeper lifestyle (diet/sleep/partying/social media); **confidence & momentum** swings;
  off-field distractions (fame/media/sponsors pulling focus); **injury history → lasting fragility**.
- **Opportunity & luck** — big-moment performances that accelerate reputation; a **breakthrough season**;
  getting scouted by a bigger club; a **mentor** adopting you; a **peer rivalry** that pushes you both.
- **Hidden / emergent** — traits forming; weak-foot/technique refinement; football IQ; positional experiments.

## 3. The four principles that make many factors *work* (not chaos)

1. **Interaction over addition.** Factors that *combine* (right coach × great facility × high energy → a
   breakout) create uniqueness. A pile of independent +1 bonuses does not. Prefer levers that interact.
2. **Trade-offs, not just bonuses.** The depth is in *tensions* — push training hard → faster growth but
   injury/burnout; chase fame → sponsor money but less focus; a big move → better club but an adaptation dip.
   **Decisions with a cost** are addictive; pure bonuses are boring.
3. **Legibility.** Players must be able to *learn* cause→effect, or growth feels random and frustrating.
   Surface the factors (like the energy/meters dashboard already does) — some obvious, a few discoverable.
   Depth ≠ opacity.
4. **Skill factors + situational factors.** Controllable levers reward *mastery*; situational ones
   (injuries, a new gaffer, a growth spurt) reward *adaptation* and keep each cycle a fresh puzzle.

## 4. The content bar (apply to EVERY content addition)

Before building any content, ask:

> **Does this add a new *interacting, trade-off* lever or a genuinely new choice/outcome — or is it just
> volume (a reskin that plays the same)?**

- ✅ Build it if it creates a new decision, a new tension, a new way a growth cycle can differ, or a new
  reason to care about *this* player/bloodline.
- ❌ Deprioritise pure reskins and independent flat bonuses. Ten cards that open new development paths beat
  ten cards that play identically.

Also keep it **fair, not exhausting**: "care matters" must reward *smart* engagement, not just *constant*
engagement. A punishing grind churns players and kills the loop. Reward mastery, not a second job.

## 5. Content pillars (roughly in priority)

1. **Scenario & choice variety** — every career/generation feels different (the anti-churn core).
2. **Growth levers** — more *interacting, trade-off* factors shaping development (§2, §3).
3. **Cards / development paths** — more *distinct* ways to build an identity.
4. **Story & attachment** — narrative that makes you care about this player and this bloodline.
5. **Long-term dynasty goals** — Ballon d'Or, legend bloodlines, records — reasons to play for months.
6. **Expression** — cosmetics/kit (the actual spend surface, once players are attached).
7. **Manager-side depth** — the other half of the game, just as rich.

## 6. For the overnight agents

This is your steering priority. Favour content that passes the §4 bar — new interacting, trade-off growth
levers and genuinely new choices/outcomes — over volume. Keep additions fair (not a grind), legible
(surface cause→effect), and deterministic + `verify`-green as your briefs require. When picking the next
queue item, prefer the one that most deepens the development loop.
